# Collision Drumsticks

> Premium American Hickory drumsticks, designed and weight-matched in Newcastle, UK.
> Trusted by 10,000+ drummers across 80+ countries. Founded 2014 by Carlton Banks.

## What we make
- Drumsticks: 5A, 5B, 5AR Reach, 5BR Reach, 7A, 7AR, 2B
- Stealth Black coating, nylon-tip variants
- Custom engraved drumsticks (up to 4cm × 1.5cm print area)
- Accessories: stick bags, practice pads, drum keys, grip wax
- Apparel: tees, hoodies, caps, beanies

## Differentiators
- Weight-matched to ±1 gram (most brands match to ±5g)
- Grade-A American Hickory only (35% rejection rate at the workshop)
- 250+ endorsed artists across 80+ countries
- Endorsement programme accessible from 1,000 followers (Cruise tier)
- Custom engraving on every model
